About Me

Who am I?

I'm Furkan Parlak, a software developer and electrical engineer.

I am a software developer and an Electrical Engineering graduate of Düzce University. At Başarsoft I build routing and optimization services (routing, isochrones, TSP/VRP, time-distance matrices) and the React front end of the RotabanV2 product.

Before that I worked as an R&D engineer in the defence industry, designing multi-layer PCBs and embedded software with STM32, PIC, RTOS and bare-metal ARM. I learn fast, adapt quickly to new technologies and enjoy working in a team.

Lodos Electromobile

Lodos is Düzce University’s electric vehicle team that competes in the TÜBİTAK Efficiency Challenge. As team captain I also worked on the car’s electronics: the vehicle control system, the battery management system and the battery charger.

Read the news article (Turkish)

What I did

  • Vehicle Control System (2018): Linux-based control panel written with Qt; lights, horn and wipers controlled by an ATmega328P over SPI; LoRa and MQTT communication.
  • Battery Management System (2019): for a 16S22P Li-ion pack, with active cell balancing, overload protection, an isolated power supply and multi-channel temperature monitoring and protection.
  • Li-ion Charger (2020): 16S, 10 A, 500 W charger based on a full-bridge buck converter topology, including the PCB design.
