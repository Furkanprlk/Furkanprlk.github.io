Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This website is operated by Furkan Parlak, who also publishes mobile games under the name Quartz Games ("we", "us"). This page explains what information is collected when you visit this website or use our apps, and how it is used.

Information we collect on this website

This website has no user accounts and no contact form. We only receive personal information that you choose to send us, for example when you e-mail us or book an appointment through the Google Calendar appointment page. We use that information only to reply to you.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web servers, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This website shows ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

More information: How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work without them.

Embedded content from other services

Some pages load content from third parties, which may collect data about your visit under their own privacy policies:

  • YouTube – the background video on the home page and linked project videos (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Fonts and Google Calendar – fonts and the appointment booking button.
  • Google Sign-In – used only on the DigiLabs early-access page. Data collected there is described in the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (Turkish).

Mobile apps (Quartz Games)

Our games do not ask you for personal information. They use third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device, such as advertising IDs, IP address and crash or usage statistics:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob

Some apps have their own policy page, for example Extreme Car Games Drift Racing – Privacy Policy. The End User License Agreement applies to all of our apps.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. To use these rights, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

This website and our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the date at the top will be updated.
